Where I would put the money

The cheap win

Cel Horde. One session, zero systems touched, and it fixes the two things that actually look bad right now — the flat colour and the two-frame flip. If you dislike it, we roll back by swapping one function.

The real upgrade

Low-Poly. This is the one that changes how the game plays, not just how it looks: zombies gain a facing direction, so flanking means something and hit location becomes readable.

The whole-scene call

Toon Shell. Gorgeous, and it drags the entire art direction with it. Worth doing — but as a deliberate reskin of the game, not as a zombie change.

The thing worth deciding first: whether zombies should have a facing direction. That is a gameplay question hiding inside an art question — and it is the only one of these choices you cannot cheaply undo later. Everything about how they look can be re-skinned in an afternoon once that is settled.

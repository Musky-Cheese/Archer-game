# V2.8 bow art target

Use `reference/v2.8-bow-art-target.png` as the target for the licensed Bow &
Arrows rig conversion. It is visual reference only and is not a game texture,
background, or source model.

Keep these visible traits:

- slender asymmetric recurve silhouette with pale reinforced tips;
- dark laminated wood with a narrow lighter edge layer;
- an angular, practical riser with a clearly readable arrow shelf;
- worn brown leather wrapping at the grip and selected limb joints;
- black serving at the nocking area and a restrained warm/cool night palette.

For the game export, retain the source asset's CC BY 3.0 attribution, use real
Blender geometry and rig motion, keep the bow under 800 triangles where
practical, and use at most one compact 256 px texture atlas.
